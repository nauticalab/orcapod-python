## `src/orcapod/side_effects.py`

### SE1 — `SideEffectPodStream.as_table` and the side-effect node table ignored or rejected column configs
**Status:** resolved
**Severity:** medium
**Issue:** ITL-1066

Both built their table by passing one `ColumnConfig` to `tag.as_table()` and `data.as_table()` and
stacking the halves: `context` / `all_info` raised on a duplicate `_context_key`, `content_hash`
and `sort_by_tags` were silently ignored.

**Fix:** one helper, `_table_of_rows`, collects every column group per row and shapes through
`arrow_utils.apply_column_config` and `with_content_hash_column`, as the function pod streams do.
Known gap left as it is (not part of this issue): the table of NO rows, here and in
`FunctionPodStream`, is typed from the declared schema and so has no `_context_key` or
`_source_*` column even when asked for.
